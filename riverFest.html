Grand River Festival - A Cultural Heritage

Experience the vibrant and sacred tradition of the Grand River Festival, where thousands gather to celebrate faith, culture, and nature.

About the Festival

This festival, celebrated on the banks of a holy river, is an ancient tradition where people gather to perform rituals, take sacred dips, and pay homage to deities.

(ई महोत्सव एगो पवित्र नदी के किनारे मनावल जाला, जहाँ हजारों श्रद्धालु एकजुट होके पूजा-पाठ आ परंपरा निभावेला।)

🐘 Elephants in the Festival

Decorated elephants add to the grandeur of this event. They are considered sacred and play an important role in processions and religious ceremonies.

(सजावल-धजावल हाथी भी एह पर्व के शान बढ़ावेला। ई धार्मिक आस्था में विशेष महत्व राखेला।)

Cultural & Religious Significance

  • 🌞 Holy Rituals: Devotees take a dip in the river, believing it washes away sins.
  • 🎶 Traditional Music & Dance: Folk performances enrich the festive spirit.
  • 🛕 Pilgrimage Destination: Many temples nearby make it an important religious hub.

📅 When & Where?

📌 Location: Sacred riverbank, Bihar, India.

Best Time to Visit: During the festival season (October - November).

How to Reach: Well-connected by road and rail
