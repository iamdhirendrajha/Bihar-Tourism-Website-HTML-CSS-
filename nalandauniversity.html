Nalanda University - A Glorious Ancient Heritage

Nalanda University is one of the world's oldest educational institutions, located in Bihar, India. It was a major center for learning from the 5th to the 12th century and attracted scholars from all over the world.

Location

Nalanda University is situated in the Nalanda district of Bihar, India, around 95 km from Patna, the state capital.

History

Founded in the 5th century during the Gupta Empire, Nalanda was an important center for Buddhist studies, attracting students and scholars from countries like China, Korea, Japan, Tibet, and Indonesia. It was destroyed in the 12th century by the Turkish invader Bakhtiyar Khilji.

How to Visit Nalanda?

You can visit Nalanda via different modes of transport:

  • By Air: The nearest airport is in Patna, about 90 km away.
  • By Train: The nearest railway station is Rajgir (12 km away), with connections to major cities.
  • By Road: Nalanda is well-connected by road, with buses and taxis available from Patna, Bodh Gaya, and Rajgir.

Best Time to Visit

The ideal time to visit Nalanda is between October and March, when the weather is pleasant for sightseeing.

Attractions Near Nalanda

  • Rajgir - An ancient city with Buddhist and Jain significance (12 km away)
  • Pawapuri - A sacred Jain pilgrimage site (20 km away)
  • Bodh Gaya - The place where Lord Buddha attained enlightenment (90 km away)

Conclusion

Visiting Nalanda is a journey into India's rich historical and cultural past. It is a must-visit for history lovers and those interested in ancient education systems.
