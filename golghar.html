Golghar - The Iconic Granary of Patna 🏛️

Golghar, located in Patna, Bihar, is a historic granary built by the British in 1786. This massive dome-shaped structure was designed by Captain John Garstin to prevent food shortages. With its unique architecture and 145 spiral steps leading to the top, Golghar offers a breathtaking panoramic view of the city and the Ganges River.

Over the years, Golghar has become a popular tourist attraction, drawing visitors who admire its architectural brilliance and historical significance. If you visit Patna, a trip to Golghar is a must to experience its charm and heritage.

Location: Near Gandhi Maidan, Patna, Bihar

Best Time to Visit: October to March
