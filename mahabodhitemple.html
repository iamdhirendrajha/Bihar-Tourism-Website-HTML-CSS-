Vishwa Shanti Stupa - Rajgir

A Symbol of Peace and Harmony

About Vishwa Shanti Stupa

Vishwa Shanti Stupa, also known as the World Peace Pagoda, is a beautiful Buddhist monument located in Rajgir, Bihar. It is one of the 80 peace pagodas built around the world by the Japanese Buddhist monk Nichidatsu Fujii. Situated at the top of Ratnagiri Hill, this white-domed stupa symbolizes global peace and non-violence.

History of Vishwa Shanti Stupa

The stupa was built in the 1960s as part of the peace movement started by Fujii Guruji. It houses four golden Buddha statues representing different stages of Buddha's life – birth, enlightenment, preaching, and death. The location is historically significant as Rajgir was the first capital of the Magadha Kingdom and an important site for Buddhist teachings.

Location

The stupa is located on Ratnagiri Hill in Rajgir, Bihar. It offers breathtaking panoramic views of the surrounding landscape, making it a must-visit tourist destination.

align How to Reach?

  • By Air: The nearest airport is Patna Airport (about 100 km away).
  • By Train: Rajgir Railway Station is the closest railhead.
  • By Road: Buses and taxis are available from Patna, Bodh Gaya, and Nalanda.
  • By Ropeway: A scenic ropeway ride is available to reach the stupa.

Major Attractions

  • Japanese Temple: A serene Buddhist temple near the stupa.
  • Griddhakuta Peak: A major Buddhist pilgrimage site.
  • Venu Van: A peaceful park believed to be Buddha’s meditation site.
  • Ajatshatru Fort: Built by King Ajatshatru of Magadha.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Rajgir and the stupa is between October and March when the weather is pleasant for sightseeing.

Important Information

  • Entry Fee: Free
  • Ropeway Ticket: ₹60 per person (approx.)
  • Timings: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
