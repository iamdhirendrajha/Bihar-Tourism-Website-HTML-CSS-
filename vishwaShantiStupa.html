Vishwa Shanti Stupa - The Peace Pagoda

Experience the serenity and spiritual aura of the Vishwa Shanti Stupa, a symbol of world peace, located on the Ratnagiri hills in Rajgir, Bihar.

About Vishwa Shanti Stupa

Also known as the World Peace Pagoda, this magnificent white stupa was built by Nipponzan Myohoji, a Japanese Buddhist organization, to spread the message of non-violence and peace.

(ई भव्य श्वेत स्तूप के जापानी बौद्ध संगठन निप्पोंजन म्योहो जी बनवले बा, जेकर उद्देश्य अहिंसा आ शांति के संदेश फैलावल बा।)

Significance & Architecture

  • Spiritual Landmark: One of the 80 peace pagodas worldwide, symbolizing harmony.
  • Stunning Structure: A white dome with four golden Buddha statues representing different phases of Buddha’s life.
  • Scenic Beauty: Located at a height of 400 meters on the Ratnagiri Hill, offering breathtaking views.

(ई स्तूप दुनिया भर के 80 शांति स्तूपन में से एक ह, जवन शांति के प्रतीक ह। इहाँ से शानदार नज़ारा देखे के मिलेला।)

How to Reach?

  • Ropeway: A thrilling ropeway ride takes visitors to the top of the hill.
  • Trekking: Pilgrims can also hike up the hill for a peace ul journey.
  • Nearest Railway Station: Rajgir Railway Station (6 km away).
  • Nearest Airport: Gaya International Airport (78 km away).
