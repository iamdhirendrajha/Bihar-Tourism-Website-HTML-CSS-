About the Statue

Experience the divine presence of Lord Buddha through this magnificent 82-feet-tall statue, a symbol of peace and enlightenment.

The Great Buddha Statue in Bodh Gaya was inaugurated by the Dalai Lama on November 18, 1989. It stands 82 feet (25 meters) tall and is surrounded by ten smaller statues of Buddha’s disciples. Made of red granite and sandstone, this statue represents Buddha in a meditative posture.

Significance

This statue symbolizes Buddha’s journey to enlightenment and is one of the most important pilgrimage sites for Buddhists worldwide. It reflects the principles of meditation, wisdom, and peace.

Visit Information

Location: Near Mahabodhi Temple, Bodh Gaya, Bihar, India.

How to Reach:

  • By Air: Gaya International Airport (12 km away).
  • By Train: Gaya Junction (16 km away).
  • By Road: Well-connected via buses and taxis from Patna, Varanasi, and other cities.

Best Time to Visit: October to March.
